Semantic system

Each role sits at its own anchor hue, pulled toward the brand only when the two are within 90°. A hue roughly opposite the brand is left alone — dragging it would produce a color that is neither harmonious nor recognisable as its role.

Semantic tuning

Neutrals

A tinted neutral is what makes an interface feel designed rather than assembled. The warm and cool pair sit ±40° off the brand hue, for surfaces that need to read apart from it.

Accents

Non-semantic hues placed in OKLCH hue space, all inheriting the family signature.

Light and dark

The dark set is generated, not inverted: the ladder is remapped so the darkest stops become surfaces, chroma comes down at high lightness where saturated light text vibrates, and the neutral tint leans further toward the brand.
