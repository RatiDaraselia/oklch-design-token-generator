Color system

One brand color, read as a structural signature, reissued across every semantic hue. Intensity is held as a share of each hue’s gamut ceiling, so the family stays consistent where absolute chroma cannot.

How the system works

Everything on this page derives from a single input. The color you paste is read not as a value but as a signature — three properties measured in OKLCH: its lightness, its intensity (chroma expressed as a share of the most that hue can hold at that lightness), and its lift (how far it sits from the lightness its hue reaches most cleanly). Those three numbers, not the hex, are what every other ramp inherits.

Holding intensity as a ratio rather than an absolute is what makes the family read as one system. Yellow can carry far more chroma than blue at the same lightness, so a fixed C 0.15 leaves one washed out and the other overdriven, while the same share of ceiling makes them siblings. Every value is gamut-checked against sRGB before it is emitted, so nothing silently clips.

Each role — brand, error, warning, success, info, tinted neutrals, and any number of accents — sits at its own anchor hue and expands across an eleven-stop lightness ladder, 50 through 950. Semantic hues are pulled toward the brand only when they fall within 90° of it; a role roughly opposite the brand is left where it is, because dragging it across the wheel produces a color that is neither harmonious nor still recognisable as error. Any stop can be locked to hold an exact value, and its neighbours re-smooth around it.

Three corrections then run on every regeneration. Roles that land within ΔEOK 0.10 of each other are rotated apart until they are perceptually distinct. Error and success are re-checked under simulated deuteranopia and separated in lightness if they collide there rather than only in full colour. And each role’s 600 stop walks darker until it clears 4.5:1 against its own 50. Every intervention is reported inline, with how far it moved and why.

The dark set is generated, not inverted: the ladder is remapped so the darkest stops become surfaces, chroma comes down at high lightness where saturated light text vibrates, and the neutral tint leans further toward the brand. Everything exports from live state — CSS custom properties with an oklch() upgrade block and optional Display-P3, Tailwind v4 @theme, SCSS maps, flat and nested JSON, DTCG tokens, and an SVG swatch sheet — and the whole system encodes into the URL, so a link reproduces it exactly.
